About us

Kamuguti Sacco is a member-owned savings and credit co-operative. We were started by local people who wanted a fair, trusted place to save and borrow. Every member has a voice and shares in our yearly dividends.

Common questions

How do I join?

Visit our office with your ID and passport photo. Fill in one form and pay the registration fee.

How do I pay my loan or savings?

Use M-Pesa Paybill 000000. Use your member number as the account number.

How do I check my balance?

Call or visit the office. We give statements on request.
